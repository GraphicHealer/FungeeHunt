# Teams

Teams are created by the Game Master on the **Teams** page — usually with **AUTO-CREATE**, which splits the unassigned players into *n* balanced teams. Each team gets:

- exactly one **Team Captain** — an app player (someone who joined on their phone), drivers preferred;
- at least one **driver** (a player who answered *Yes* to "Will you have a car available to drive?");
- an even share of the remaining players.

The Game Master can also add or edit teams by hand. See [Players & Teams](../game-master/players-teams.md) for the GM side.

## Team Captain

The captain's phone is the team's camera. Only the captain sees the **Take Photo…** / **Submit** buttons and only their submissions count. Everyone else on the team sees the same task list, team roster and rules read-only, so they can help plan.

When the game starts the captain gets a **You are the Team Captain** popup reminding them to keep their phone charged.

## Team tab

The **Team** tab on the player's phone lists the Team Captain and all members, including offline players the Game Master added. Team names default to *Team 1*, *Team 2*, … unless the Game Master renames them.

## Points

A team's score is the sum of points from approved task submissions, plus any **return bonus** and **food drive** points the Game Master records on the dashboard. The team's own total is shown at the top of the **Team** tab; the full leaderboard is on the [spectator view](./spectator.md) and the GM dashboard.
